Retail Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to uncover business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and revenue growth.
The analysis helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions by identifying key sales patterns and opportunities for improvement.

Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Evaluate customer purchasing behavior.
* Track sales trends over time.
* Discover revenue-driving factors.
* Provide actionable business recommendations.

Dataset Information

The dataset contains retail transaction records, including:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Customer Information
* Product Category
* Product Name
* Quantity Sold
* Unit Price
* Sales Amount
* Region/Location
* Payment Method

Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Excel
* SQL
* Power BI
* Git & GitHub

Analysis Performed

Sales Performance Analysis

* Total Revenue

* Monthly Sales Trend

Product Analysis

* Best-Selling Products
* Top Revenue-Generating Categories
* Product Performance Comparison

Customer Analysis

* Customer Purchase Frequency
* High-Value Customers
* Customer Segmentation

Regional Analysis

* Sales by Region
* Regional Revenue Comparison
* Market Performance Insights

Key Insights

* Identified top-performing products contributing most of the revenue.
* Found seasonal sales trends affecting business performance.
* Highlighted customer segments with high purchase frequency.
* Determined regions with the highest sales growth potential.

Dashboard Features

* Interactive Filters and Slicers
* Sales Trend Visualization
* Product Performance Dashboard
* Regional Sales Analysis
* KPI Cards for Revenue, Orders, and Customers

Business Recommendations

1. Increase inventory for high-demand products.
2. Focus marketing efforts on top-performing regions.
3. Improve retention strategies for repeat customers.
4. Launch targeted promotions during low-sales periods.
5. Monitor product performance regularly for optimization.
